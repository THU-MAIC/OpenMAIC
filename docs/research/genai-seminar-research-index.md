## Core findings at a glance

1. **Access ≠ impact.** Natural-language interfaces democratise usage, but fluency and support determine who benefits.
2. **Cost of error, not error rate, should drive adoption.** Many AI tasks can be deployed with bounded, detectable error.
3. **AI tutors can outperform active-learning classrooms** in specific, well-scaffolded conditions (PS2 RCT), but generalisability is uncertain.
4. **AI risks widening educational gaps** unless products explicitly build literacy, scaffolding, and accountability.
5. **Teacher roles must shift** from content delivery to higher-order facilitation; this requires institutional redesign, not just tools.
