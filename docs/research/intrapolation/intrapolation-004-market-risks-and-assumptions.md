# Intrapolation-004 — Market Risks & Assumption Checks

**Date:** 2026-06-04  
**Research Phase:** Intrapolation — internal critique of market research

---

## 1. Market Sizing Uncertainty

| Source | India EdTech 2025 | 2030/2034 Forecast |
|--------|-------------------|--------------------|
| RAYSolute | $2.8–3.0 Bn D2C online/hybrid | $5–7 Bn by 2030 |
| IMARC | $3.63 Bn total | $33.3 Bn by 2034 (CAGR 27.94 %) |
| IndiaMarketEntry | B2B layer “almost entirely untouched” | — |

**Risk:** Forecasts vary widely. The conservative Dr. Math planning assumption should be **$3 Bn near-term market**, with upside if institutional B2B accelerates.

---

## 2. Trust Deficit

- BYJU’S collapse created **FOGS** (Fear of Getting Scammed).[^7]
- Parents and principals are skeptical of long-term subscriptions and unverified claims.
- **Mitigation:** month-to-month pricing, transparent outcome dashboards, teacher-in-the-loop controls, no loans.

---

## 3. Competitive Intensity

- **PhysicsWallah** has brand, distribution, and capital; it could enter the worksheet/OMR space.
- **Classplus/Teachmint** already own coaching-centre workflows.
- **LEAD** owns school workflows.
- **Mitigation:** be the best AI grading + adaptive worksheet module; integrate rather than replace.

---

## 4. Regulatory

- **DPDP Act 2023** requires consent for minors’ data; school/tuition centres may be data fiduciaries.
- **NEP 2020** is supportive but implementation is uneven across states.
- **Mitigation:** publish a one-page DPDP compliance note; keep data in India by default; avoid cross-border transfers.

---

## 5. Technical / Product Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| LLM math errors | Wrong answers destroy trust | Symbolic validation layer + human spot-checks |
| OMR accuracy on poor scans | Grading failures | Multiple fallback modes (manual entry, QR code) |
| Vernacular generation quality | Confusing questions | Native-speaker review before release |
| Low internet / device access | Usage drop-off | Lightweight PDF-first, offline worksheets |

---

## 6. What We Did Not Research

- Exact procurement cycles of Indian private schools.
- State-government DIKSHA/PM SHRI tender processes.
- Competitive pricing from IntelGrader, Ginger Webs OMR, and other AI-grading players.
- Parent willingness-to-pay primary survey data.

---

## 7. Conclusion

The market opportunity is **real but noisy**. Dr. Math should prioritise **B2B2C tuition centres**, keep pricing flexible and low-friction, and build trust through measurable outcomes. The research supports moving forward with a pilot, not a large upfront B2C launch.
