## 3. Funding & Consolidation

- Indian EdTech funding rebounded to **$120 M in H1 2025**, a 5× surge vs prior year, led by PhysicsWallah and AI tutoring startups.[^10]
- Global EdTech M&A hit landmark deals in 2025: Coursera-Udemy ($2.5 Bn), KKR-Instructure ($4.8 Bn), Bain-PowerSchool ($5.6 Bn).[^11]
- India-specific consolidation signals: upGrad-Unacademy, PW school-centre programme, LEAD School expansion.

---

## 4. Consumer Psychology Shift

RAYSolute describes the shift from **FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)** to **FOGS (Fear of Getting Scammed)** after the BYJU’S collapse.[^7] Parents now demand:

- Tangible outcomes and progress proof.
- No aggressive loan-based sales.
- Teacher / principal visibility into the product.
- Value-for-money pricing.

---

## 5. Regulatory Tailwinds & Headwinds

- **NEP 2020** pushes digital learning, competency-based assessment, and school integration.[^14]
- **Dummy-school crackdown** and **16-year rule** for coaching enrollments constrain test-prep marketing.[^7]
- **DPDP Act 2023** raises the bar for student data consent and cross-border transfer.

---

## 6. Strategic Takeaways

1. **B2C is a two-horse race** (PhysicsWallah vs upGrad-Unacademy); new entrants should avoid head-on competition.
2. **Institutional B2B is underpenetrated:** 1.5 M schools, only a few thousand digitally served.
3. **Outcome evidence is becoming a procurement requirement** for large buyers.
4. **Vernacular, low-bandwidth, teacher-in-the-loop** products have the widest addressable market.
