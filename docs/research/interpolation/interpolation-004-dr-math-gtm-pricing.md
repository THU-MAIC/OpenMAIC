# Interpolation-004 — Dr. Math Go-to-Market & Pricing

**Date:** 2026-06-04  
**Research Phase:** Interpolation — applying market findings to Dr. Math product and pricing

---

## 1. Target Segments (Priority Order)

| Segment | Why First? | Entry Price Signal |
|---------|------------|--------------------|
| **Tuition centres / coaching chains** | Centralized buyer, immediate worksheet need, low switching cost | ₹50–150 / student / month |
| **Budget private schools** | NEP compliance pressure, parent demand for progress proof | ₹10–50 / student / month as add-on |
| **Individual tutors / micro-hubs** | Viral word-of-mouth, low CAC | Freemium; paywall after 50 students |
| **Large B2B school OS partners** | Distribution at scale | Revenue-share or per-student API fee |

---

## 2. Pricing Experiments

### Experiment A: Tuition Centre “AI Worksheet Pack”

- ₹999/month base + ₹30/student/month.
- Includes: 4 adaptive worksheets/month, OMR scanning, parent WhatsApp report.
- Annual commit: 2 months free.

### Experiment B: School “RTI Lab” Add-On

- ₹15/student/month (annual billing).
- Includes: class-level gap analysis, remedial worksheet packets, teacher dashboard.
- Pilot one grade free for one term.

### Experiment C: Free Tier for Validation

- Natalmanjaya High School continues as a free design partner.
- Use anonymised outcome data for case studies and sales collateral.

---

## 3. Packaging Suggestions

| Tier | Monthly Price | Features |
|------|---------------|----------|
| **Starter** | Free | Up to 50 students, 2 worksheets/month, basic PDF |
| **Growth** | ₹2,499–4,999/mo | Unlimited students, OMR scan, adaptive next worksheet, analytics |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Multi-branch, white-label app, API access, priority support |

These bands mirror ECartpe/Classplus coaching-software pricing.[^12][^18]

---

## 4. Go-to-Market Motion

1. **Reference customer:** Natalmanjaya High School + 2–3 local tuition centres.
2. **Organic channels:** Teacher/principal WhatsApp groups, local education fairs, YouTube shorts in Hindi.
3. **Sales enablement:** One-page case study showing “before/after” marks improvement (with consent).
4. **Partnerships:** Integrate with Classplus/Teachmint via export/import; later pursue API partnerships.

---

## 5. Metrics to Track

- Cost per centre acquired (target < ₹5,000).
- Monthly worksheets generated per student.
- OMR scan success rate and grading accuracy.
- Student outcome delta (pre/post topic mastery).
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) across centres.
