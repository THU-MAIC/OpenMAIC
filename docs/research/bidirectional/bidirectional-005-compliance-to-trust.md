# Bidirectional-005 — From Compliance to Trust

**Date:** 2026-06-04  
**Research Phase:** Bidirectional — cross-connect DPDP compliance with market trust and sales

---

## 1. The Trust Deficit

Post-BYJU’S, Indian parents and school principals operate under **FOGS** (Fear of Getting Scammed).[^7] DPDP compliance is not just a legal shield — it is a **sales and retention tool**.

---

## 2. Compliance → Trust Mapping

| DPDP Obligation | Parent/Principal Fear Addressed | Sales Message |
|-----------------|---------------------------------|---------------|
| Verifiable parental consent | “Will you sign my child up without asking me?” | “No student data is processed until the school/parent consents.” |
| Data minimization | “Will you collect Aadhaar, photos, or location?” | “We only store what is needed to generate the worksheet.” |
| India hosting | “Will my child’s data leave the country?” | “All student data stays in India by default.” |
| No behavioral ads / tracking | “Will you sell or monetize my child’s data?” | “We do not advertise to students or sell data.” |
| Breach notification | “What happens if there is a leak?” | “We have a 72-hour breach-response plan and encryption.” |
| Right to erasure | “Can I delete my child’s data?” | “One-click export and deletion for schools and parents.” |

---

## 3. Institutional Procurement Advantage

Schools and tuition centres are increasingly asked by parents about data practices. A **one-page DPDP compliance note** can shorten procurement evaluation and differentiate Dr. Math from vendors who treat compliance as an afterthought.

---

## 4. Recommended Trust Artifacts

1. Public privacy policy in English + Hindi.
2. One-page DPDP compliance summary for principals.
3. Data Processing Addendum (DPA) template.
4. Annual third-party security audit (post-MVP).
5. Consent dashboard for parents/teachers.
