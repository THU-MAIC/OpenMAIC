---

## 2. What Global Buyers Want

- **Sticky enterprise / institutional contracts**
- **Defensible AI moats**
- **Verifiable learner outcomes**
- **Workflow embedding** (LMS, SIS, assessment, communication)

---

## 3. India as an Institutional Market

IndiaMarketEntry argues the **real opportunity for US/UK EdTech providers is institutional**, not consumer:[^9]

- 1.5 M schools, 52,321 colleges, 16,000+ preschools.
- Only ~0.6 % of schools are meaningfully digitally served.
- Domestic consumer platforms have cultural/vernacular advantages; international providers can compete on curriculum, assessment tools, teacher PD, and LMS.

## 4. Bidirectional Implications for Dr. Math

| Global Trend | India Application |

| K-12 workflow embedding | Integrate with existing school ERP / LMS via APIs |
| AI assessment platforms | OMR scan + AI grading layer for worksheets |
| Skills verification | Track mastery per topic, generate portable progress reports |

| Platform consolidation | Partner with or become the AI worksheet module inside larger school OS |

---

## 5. Strategic Options

1. **Standalone B2B SaaS:** Sell directly to tuition chains and budget private schools.
2. **Embed inside Classplus/Teachmint:** Become a worksheet/grading plugin.
3. **White-label for school OS partners:** LEAD, NextOS, etc.
4. **Outcome-data layer:** Sell analytics/RTI dashboards to existing coaching chains.
