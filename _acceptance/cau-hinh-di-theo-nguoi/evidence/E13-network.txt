ROOT CAUSE of the 404s on /api/persistence/kv/entries/*, /api/stages, /api/folders:
  no DATABASE_URL is configured for this dev server (repo has no local PostgreSQL running,
  psql/pg_isready are not even installed on this machine). app/api/persistence/[...path]/route.ts
  and the stages/folders routes require a real Postgres pool (lib/persistence/server-provider.ts,
  @openmaic/storage/*/pg) and fail closed with no fallback. This is the exact, pre-disclosed
  limitation named in this eval's own Expected text: "Máy đo KHÔNG có PostgreSQL nên luồng
  đầu-cuối không chạy trọn được". Mint/redeem (/api/claim, /api/claim/redeem) are in-memory
  and do NOT depend on Postgres, and both returned 200 in every live attempt.

CONSEQUENCE for the redeem->adopt flow (verified live, unmocked, in a real click-through):
  entering a real code minted by a separate identity and clicking "Replace them" DOES burn the
  code (redeem succeeds) but the subsequent rehydrate step (reloadAccountStoresAndConfirm ->
  zustand persist.rehydrate -> HttpKVStore GET) fails against the DB-less backend, so the UI
  lands on ST-maycuatoi-may-chu-im ("Could not reach the server. Your current choices are
  unchanged.") instead of ST-maycuatoi-xong. This was reproduced twice with two different fresh
  codes from two different (cookie-cleared) browser identities.
